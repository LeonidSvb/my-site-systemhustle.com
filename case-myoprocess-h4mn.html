Clean-Seas West Virginia — Industrial plastic conversion6 intros / 120 days

A plastic-conversion facility in Belle, WV needed qualified industrial relationships around feedstock, offtake and logistics — not a volume lead list. The market was slow and conservative: every introduction had to survive the question "can this party actually supply, buy, route, or finance the project?" Over 120 days, the route produced 6 qualified introductions.

Why it's relevant here: the same buyer profile as trade-compliance decision-makers — conservative, high qualification bar, one right introduction worth more than a hundred generic replies.

Vention — Manufacturing automation+$85K / quarter

Vention's platform helps manufacturers design, order and assemble custom industrial equipment. The team wasn't reaching decision-makers in manufacturing and automation consistently, and was burning budget on PPC that didn't fix the underlying targeting problem. A targeted, pain-point-driven outreach system replaced that spend and added $85K in new revenue in one quarter.

Why it's relevant here: the exact job titles you sell drawback programs into — plastics, chemicals, aerospace, automotive manufacturers.
