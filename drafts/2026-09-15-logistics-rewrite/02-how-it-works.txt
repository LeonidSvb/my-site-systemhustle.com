How It Works

I watch for real signals
I map every company in your niche, then track industry news and portals for what's actually changing — a new regulation, an expansion, a supplier switch. STEP 2:
I reach out to the person who can say yes
Not a job title. A real person with the authority to actually make this decision.

STEP 3:
If they're interested, I introduce you directly
You pick it up from there. No middlemen, no scripts, no fake urgency — just a warm intro to someone who already said yes to talking.
