SECTION TITLE:
Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What if it doesn't work?
A: The small upfront amount isn't a retainer — it's what it takes to run a focused search for your specific market and find the right approach. If nothing comes of it, that's where it ends. I only make real money if you actually close a deal.

Q: Do I need to sign a long-term contract?
A: No. It starts as a short pilot, then we both decide if it makes sense to keep going.

Q: What do you need from me to get started?
A: A short call to understand your ideal customer, your typical deal size, and how you currently land new accounts. That's it.

Q: How is this different from just hiring an agency?
A: Most agencies send generic messages to a list of job titles and hope something sticks. I only reach out when I've spotted a real signal that a company needs what you do right now, and I go to a real person, not a title.
