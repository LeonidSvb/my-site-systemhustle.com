A: cheapest to build, zero JS, loads instantly, fully accessible, works identically on mobile. Reads as a factual spec sheet — matches the "industrial/manifest" tone of the rest of the site. Downside: static, doesn't visually signal "this updates / this is live data."

B: ticker for titles = infinite horizontal scroll (reads "we know this whole role landscape"). Signals rotate one-at-a-time via fade (reads "these are live/ongoing," reinforces the signal-based positioning). Costs: needs JS/CSS-animation care on mobile (marquees can jank on low-end phones), and a lead skimming fast may miss a signal mid-fade — worse for scanability than a static list.

Cheapest of the three to keep consistent across niches since chip count naturally varies without breaking layout — a niche with 5 signals doesn't need special-casing the way a fixed 3-slide carousel would.
